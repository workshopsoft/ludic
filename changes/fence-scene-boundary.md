**The alloc fence has a documented scene boundary.** A game that builds its world after a long idle screen (a launcher past `R3D_ALLOC_WARM`, 600 frames) is already being judged, so the world build's one-time allocations failed the run (exit 86) at the first world frame. No runtime change was needed: after the build, call `Mem.play()` then `Mem.settled()`. `Mem.play()` while tracking re-arms the warm-up and the frame is not judged; `Mem.settled()` re-baselines at once, so the build's kept memory is the new floor and a real leak after it still fails at its own frame. `Mem.play()` alone (before or after the build) also works, with `R3D_ALLOC_REWARM` (120) frames of blindness. `examples/lang/alloc_fence_scene.ludic` and its case in `tools/ludic-cli/test.ludic` prove both.
