**`ludic.roads`: design heights, levels, structures, 16,384 roads and a fast nearest road.** `RoadSeg.heights` (one `RoadHeight { y, free }` per anchor, as `handles`) and `level` (`ground`, `structure`, `tunnel`) put a road above or below another at the same (x, z); `roads_seg_height`, `roads_seg_level`, `roads_seg_designed` and `roads_pose_y` read them, and cars are placed at their road's design height (a road with none follows the ground as before). New `RoadStructures` registry (`roads/structures.lres`: seg, from, to, kind bridge/viaduct/flyover/tunnel, type beam/arch/cable/box, over, deck_w, deck_t, parapet, pier_spacing, gaps) and `RoadProfile.median`, `shoulder` and `max_grade`. A map may have 16,384 roads (was 4,000; a turn is keyed `in * 32768 + out`). `roads_nearest` goes through a grid over the polylines instead of every step of every road, with the same answers, and `roads_nearest_y(x, y, z)` counts the gap in height, so under a flyover it is the road below. All new fields are defaulted, so an older map reads and drives as it did; `studio.lres` binds them for Ludic Studio.
